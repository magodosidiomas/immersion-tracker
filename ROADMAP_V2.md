# 🗺️ Imerso v2 Roadmap — Mentoring & Accountability Tools

Features and architectural enhancements to support mentor-student accountability workflows.

---

## 🎯 Mentoring Use Case Integration
> **Objective:** Turn Imerso into the proof-of-work tool for language mentoring students, eliminating the mentor bottleneck of manually checking/nagging students while avoiding notification blindness.

---

## 🚀 Key v2 Features

### 1. Student Immersion Proof & Sharing
- **Weekly Progress Card:** Generates a visually clean, exportable/shareable progress card (PNG or text summary) showing:
  - Total immersion hours logged this week.
  - Practice distribution (e.g. 70% Listening, 20% Reading, 10% Anki).
  - Weekly consistency (e.g. 6 out of 7 days active).
- **1-Click Share:** Student can tap "Compartilhar com Mentor" to send their summary via WhatsApp or email.

### 2. Consistency & Streak Tracking (Anti-Burnout)
- Focus on **consistency** (e.g. at least 30 min/day) rather than total raw hours.
- Visual heatmap/streak calendar so mentor and student can immediately spot gaps.

### 3. Mentor View / Cohort Dashboard (Future v3 Concept)
- Lightweight read-only dashboard allowing mentors to see all active students' weekly logged hours in one aggregated view without manual check-ins.
